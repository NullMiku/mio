allocation/GC. That is development scaffolding only: E4 cannot PASS until SATB,
concurrent allocation/Minor and full write-site coverage replace those guards.

at36; bit40 remembered. Any proposed bit41 Old color must be verified against
all masks, set only on Old (otherwise Young's full right-shift age is polluted),
and updated with CAS so postwrite remembered RMW cannot lose color. Resetting

without that frontier is incorrect. Promotion must be born black during a real
concurrent cycle. Legacy Major cannot free objects underneath a marker.

remaining edges. Existing generic close is suitable for the completed queue
fixture but is not by itself the complete mark termination protocol.
